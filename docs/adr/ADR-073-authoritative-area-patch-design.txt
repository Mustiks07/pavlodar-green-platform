ADR-073: Authoritative area-patch design and calculation
Status: accepted
Date: 2026-11-02

Context
F-PLANT-04 requires more than isolated tree points: shrubs, perennials and grass are designed as
areas. AreaPatch was already part of the canonical revision, but the product could neither draw it
nor include it in cost, survival, suitability, care or ecosystem calculations. Treating the patch as
one plant, guessing its area in the browser, or silently treating a missing density as zero would
make the saved design and the calculation disagree.

Decision
Contract 1.17.1 keeps the existing AreaPatch shape and registers the blocking
`area_patch_density_missing` gap. The specialist can switch between point and area planting, choose
a shrub, perennial or grass, draw or redraw a polygon, set planting date and explicit plants-per-m2
density, lock it, and delete it. Stable patch ids remain part of immutable scenario revisions.

The server is authoritative. It rejects open, self-intersecting, empty, out-of-zone or non-positive
polygons and computes metric area in EPSG:32643. Quantity is metric area multiplied by the explicit
density. That quantity feeds material, planting, delivery, reserve, replacement, survival,
suitability, care targeting and ecosystem metrics. A missing density preserves the geometry but
leaves quantity-dependent results incomplete with a typed gap; it is never converted to zero. The
client only previews topology and never presents a client-computed area, quantity or price as a
saved result.

Consequences
Project variants can now express real planting masses as well as individual trees without changing
the immutable revision model. Existing point-only scenarios remain byte-compatible and calculate as
before. MultiPolygon patches remain readable and calculable, while the current editor redraws one
Polygon at a time; splitting a multi-part patch is a future explicit editing operation rather than an
implicit geometry rewrite.
