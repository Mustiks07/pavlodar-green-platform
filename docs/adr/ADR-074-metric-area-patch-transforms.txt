ADR-074: Metric area-patch transforms and frozen-building collision
Status: accepted
Date: 2026-11-02

Context
F-3D-03 requires move, rotate, undo, redo and lock for designed objects. After ADR-073 an area
planting could be drawn again, but it could not be moved or rotated as one object, and completed
patch edits bypassed undo/redo. A transformed shrub or grass polygon could also overlap a building
even though point placements already used the exact frozen building versions.

Decision
The area editor keeps a bounded independent history for the complete AreaPatch array. Create,
redraw, species, date, density, lock, delete, move and rotation are reviewable steps. A selected
unlocked patch can be explicitly put in move mode and dragged once per history step, nudged in
metres, or rotated around its local metric centre in 15-degree steps (Alt uses one degree). Polygon
and MultiPolygon rings, including holes, transform together; the stable id and all non-geometry
properties remain unchanged. Vertex redraw remains deliberately limited to a simple Polygon.

These browser transforms are editing aids only. The saved geometry is validated by the server and
all authoritative areas and quantities are recalculated there. Contract 1.17.2 registers
`area_patch_building_conflict`. A new or geometrically changed patch is tested against building
records from the exact published DatasetVersions frozen into the proposed revision. Interior overlap
returns 422 at the exact patch geometry path; an exact edge touch remains valid. An unchanged
historical patch is grandfathered so that adding a later building input cannot block unrelated edits.

Consequences
The 2D and pitched 3D planning views now manipulate the same stable area object without inventing a
rotation field or client-owned result. Rejected local geometry remains visible in red and no child
revision is created. Patch-to-patch overlap is not prohibited: layered planting designs can be valid,
and any future exclusion rule must be explicit and versioned rather than assumed geometrically.
