ADR-005 — Research delivery is a versioned staging input
Status: accepted for internal persistence; API additions proposed for Claude review.
Date: 2026-10-29

Problem
The delivered research schemas are not canonical contracts. Plant requires fields
that cannot truthfully be inferred from genus-only candidates, nullable risk traits,
undated supplier stock and missing local growth curves. Publishing those records
directly would imply approval and make incomplete costs look complete.

Decision implemented without changing contract 1.0.0
Archive checksum-verified allowed bytes, including original units, observation and
check dates. Immutable identities, record versions and occurrences preserve source
lineage; DR-02/05 stock has one identity/version with two delivery occurrences.
Store converted values separately with exact 0.3048 factor and original ft bounds.
The adapter is pinned to this reviewed delivery, not a generic future validator.
No external fetching, no tariff PDF, no legal activation, no invented measurements.
All seven DatasetVersions are needs_review; current_version_id stays null.
QualityReport.valid_count means structurally valid, never accepted for a decision.
QualityReport.publish_allowed=false and server publication returns DataGap blockers.
City uses the OSM working contour with boundary_status=working, a real version ID
and no assertion of official extent. Sources/quality are readable to authorized
specialists. Missing utilities still block placement; missing costs do not block
reading sources, drawing a draft zone or saving an empty scenario.
Legal revisions/paragraphs/conditions remain raw claims pending primary-source and
expert review; #299/33 and greenery #52/4 are distinct. No 1.2m setback is inferred
from the trunk-circle diameter. Ricinus risk is not recorded as a legal prohibition.

Proposed next canonical change, requiring coordinated generator/client update
Read-only paginated staging-record and source-version endpoints, bounded by city and
dataset grant, with record kind, external/internal/version IDs, typed raw values,
units, observed_at, checked_at, applicability, source refs and review reasons.
Do not weaken Plant requirements or reuse species IDs for unresolved genera.
Use existing DataGap / eligibility / Metric / CostResult semantics for operation
readiness. Expose only module-specific blockers; no global 'research ready' boolean.
ADR-002/003 direction acknowledged by Claude. ADR-006 records remaining corrections
to ADR-004; do not regenerate 1.1.0 before that acknowledgement.

Correction after complete operation inventory review
transitionScenario already exists in canonical 1.0.0 (specialist baseline, admin
for approval). The initial claim that the operation was absent was incorrect.
It is now implemented for draft/review/archive with If-Match, idempotency and audit.
Approval is explicitly rejected with critical DataGap blockers until WP-05 supplies
verified calculation/safety evidence. No additional approval endpoint is needed.

Acceptance in this increment
Repeat import preserves counts/IDs; mirrored stock deduplicates; immutable staging;
unknown quantity stays null and supplier zero stays zero; no source date substitution;
unresolved rates/rules inactive; incomplete CostResult.total=null and exact known
subtotal; source/API access checks and publication denial; restart then browser read.
Map tiles, full import jobs, staging browser and domain calculation endpoints remain
work packages with acceptance outstanding, not removed from the product scope.
