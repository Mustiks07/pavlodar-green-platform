ADR-067: Forecast-versus-observed keeps original survival separate from replacements
Status: accepted
Date: 2026-11-02

Context
Replacement cohorts were stored with correct lineage, but the forecast-versus-observed response exposed
only the selected original inspection. The UI therefore could not prove the mandatory rule that replacing
a dead original plant must not increase the survival rate of the initial cohort.

Decision
Contract 1.14.0 adds an outcome summary to ForecastObservedResult: initial_quantity,
observed_alive_initial, replacement_cohort_count, replacements_planted, replacements_alive and total_alive.
The original survival probability continues to use only the original cohort inspection.

The server walks descendant replacement cohorts through replaces_cohort_id. A replacement contributes to
replacements_alive only when its latest inspection covers the complete cohort and has no unknown outcome.
If any replacement lacks such an inspection, replacements_alive and total_alive are null and a non-blocking
comparison_replacement_observation_incomplete DataGap is returned. Planted replacements are never assumed
alive. total_alive is available only when both the original and every replacement outcome are complete.

Consequences
The UI presents four separate cards: initially planted, original plants alive, replacements alive and total
alive. In the acceptance case, 4 initial plants with 3 original survivors and 1 observed live replacement
show 75% original survival and 4 total alive. The replacement restores total stock but does not rewrite the
original outcome.
