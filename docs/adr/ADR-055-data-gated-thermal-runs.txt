ADR-055: Data-gated thermal runs without fabricated output
Status: accepted
Date: 2026-11-01

Context
startThermalRun was canonical but absent. No approved local runtime/calibration bundle for
InVEST Air or SOLWEIG TMRT has been delivered.

Decision
Accept a thermal job only when its published weather dataset and approved parameter coefficient
are both authorized, belong to the scenario city, and are frozen in the scenario revision. The
durable worker currently records an unavailable SpatialResult with the exact missing runtime
reason instead of emitting invented temperatures or rasters.

Consequences
The operation, queue, lineage, cancellation/recovery envelope and result retrieval now work.
Complete thermal metrics and artifacts remain data- and adapter-gated; absence is explicit and is
never represented as zero.
