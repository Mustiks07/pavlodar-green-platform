ADR-078: Separate illustrative 3D growth from analytical results
Status: accepted
Date: 2026-11-02

Context
F-3D-04 requires the 0/1/3/5/10/20-year growth control and forbids an artistic animation from
changing analytical values. The admitted Pavlodar catalog has initial dimensions but no accepted
species age curves. Treating generic mature dimensions or foreign reference ranges as local growth
would manufacture biological precision; omitting the control entirely leaves the 3D journey
incomplete.

Decision
The project scene exposes the six required horizons as an explicitly named “development sketch”.
It applies a bounded monotonic presentation multiplier only to Three.js planting meshes. The source
Placement dimensions, scenario revision, calculation requests, shadows and exports are untouched.
The control states “3D only — calculation unchanged” and its year is stored in the URL independently
from the shadow date/time. The multiplier is code-owned presentation state, not a catalog trait or
model coefficient.

Consequences
Reviewers can inspect spatial maturity and occlusion at the required horizons without mistaking the
animation for a forecast. When an admitted age-curve contract and data version become available,
the presentation multiplier can be replaced by server-versioned dimensions; until then no ecological
or financial claim depends on this sketch.
