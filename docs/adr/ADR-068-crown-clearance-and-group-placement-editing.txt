ADR-068: Known crown footprints are authoritative placement geometry
Status: accepted
Date: 2026-11-02

Context
The scenario editor could add, move, delete and lock individual plantings, but a dense plan required
repeating every action. More importantly, two placements with saved crown dimensions could occupy the
same footprint. The client could warn, but a client-only rule would not protect API callers or reloads.

Decision
Contract 1.14.1 registers placement_spacing_conflict. Before a revision is stored, the server projects
placements with a positive saved initial_crown_radius_m and a non-unknown dimension basis into
EPSG:32643. The center distance must be at least the sum of the two radii. Exact tangency passes; both
position paths are returned when crowns overlap. Unknown radii remain unknown and never block saving.

The check is only the geometry of recorded crown footprints. It is not an agronomic spacing norm and
does not imply underground-network clearance. Building conflicts remain a separate frozen-dataset check.

The editor adds modifier/checkbox multi-selection, group lock/unlock/delete, protected deletion of locked
placements, undo/redo for group changes and optional visual axis snapping. It previews known crown
overlap, preserves the local draft after the authoritative 422, and highlights every rejected placement.
No rotation control is added: the current canonical Placement and schematic 3D model have no orientation,
so such a control would claim a state the server cannot store or render meaningfully.

Consequences
Dense plans can be edited as a group without weakening immutable revision semantics. A saved plan cannot
contain overlapping known crown footprints through any client. Plans with missing dimensions remain
editable without invented defaults and can be completed when measured or catalog-backed dimensions exist.
